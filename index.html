🙏 About This Project

Honouring Our Heritage

इतिहास Anime reimagines the greatest figures from Indian history as anime characters — with deep respect for historical accuracy. Every character is portrayed as the noble hero they were. Every detail, from Chanakya's shaved head and shikha to Shivaji Maharaj's Maratha pheta, has been carefully researched.

Our mission is to make Indian history exciting and accessible to a new generation, blending the visual appeal of anime with the richness of Bharatiya Itihaas.
